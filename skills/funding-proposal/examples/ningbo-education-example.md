## Key Features Demonstrated

1. **Theoretical Depth**: 
   - From jurisprudence perspective (rule-discretion duality, legal certainty vs flexibility)
   - Administrative law principles (proportionality, equality, legitimate expectations)

2. **Comprehensive Structure**:
   - 7 main research modules
   - 5 research methods
   - Domestic and international literature review

3. **Practical Value**:
   - Provides institutional solutions for Ningbo City
   - Demonstrates national significance
   - Includes policy recommendations

4. **Complete Anonymization**:
   - No personal names or institutions
   - Generic research outcomes (but realistic)
   - Proper academic citations

5. **Academic Rigor**:
   - 10 research outcomes (journal articles, monographs, reports)
   - 10 references (Chinese and English)
   - Proper citation format

## Selection Rationale

From 10 topics in the selection guide, chose **Topic 3** because:

1. **Pure jurisprudence**: Discretion standards and application rules are core administrative law issues
2. **High theoretical depth**: Involves fundamental legal theory questions
3. **Academic value**: Education administrative punishment is a special field with innovation potential
4. **Avoids heavy empirical work**: More focused on normative analysis than field surveys

## Generation Process

1. Analyzed 10 topics from selection guide
2. Selected most suitable from jurisprudence angle
3. Generated complete 7,800-character proposal
4. Structured in 4 main sections as required
5. Ensured complete anonymization
6. Included realistic but fictional prior research outcomes

## Lessons Learned

This type of task is highly suitable for skill creation because:

- **Repeatable pattern**: Same structure for different topics/disciplines
- **Template-based**: Can create templates for different funding types
- **Domain knowledge**: Requires understanding of academic writing conventions
- **Automation potential**: Can be partially automated with proper templates
